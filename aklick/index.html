aKlick

The easiest way to get your buddies together in real life.

One plan.
Everyone there.

aKlick turns “we should hang out” into a real plan. Pick a place, invite everyone with one link, see who’s in, then go. From Tuesday coffee to trips abroad.

Open aKlick

Why we built it

Built to get people back in the same room

The tools that were supposed to bring us closer mostly taught us to scroll. Group chats fill up and nobody goes anywhere. aKlick is built for the opposite: the plan gets made, everyone shows up, and the time together happens in person.

1

Find a spot everyone’s happy with

Search nearby by mood, like vegan, coffee or a park. Not sure? Tap “Help me choose” and get spots suggested nearby, anywhere in the world.

  • Distance, hours and price at a glance
  • Parks, restaurants, nights out, whole cities
  • Save favourites for next time
2

Make the plan in seconds

Pick a day, a time and a place, then send the invite. Add stops to turn one coffee into a full day out.

  • Invite anyone with one link, no account needed
  • Add stops for a full day out
  • Tuesday coffee or two weeks abroad
3

Know who’s actually coming

Everyone taps Going, Maybe or Can’t. The plan card shows who’s in at a glance, so nobody has to scroll back through the chat to count heads.

  • Clear answers, no endless back-and-forth
  • Who’s coming, right on the plan card
  • Hourly weather for the time you picked
4

One plan, one chat, everything in it

Every plan gets its own chat with polls, a checklist, photos, calls and the weather. Parking and directions are built in. Nothing scattered across five apps.

  • Its own chat, with voice and video calls
  • Polls and a shared checklist
  • Parking marked, directions built in
5

Check in with one tap

Arrived? One tap lets the group know you’re there. See who’s already waiting and who’s still on the way. No more “where are you?” texts.

  • One tap and the group knows
  • See who’s arrived and who’s on the way
  • Works at every stop of a day out
6

Every plan, right on your calendar

See all your plans in a 3-day, month or year view, side by side with the calendars you already use.

  • 3-day, month and year views
  • Sits next to the calendars you link
  • No more double-booking your Friday night

See it in action

No app? No problem.

Send one link to anyone. They see the place, the day and who’s coming before they decide. No account needed.

Private by default

No phone number. No real name. Safe for kids, and compliant with privacy law wherever you are.

Works wherever you are

Use it on Android, iPhone, iPad or the web at aklick.ai. Your plans and chats stay in sync on every device.
