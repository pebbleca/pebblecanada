Good to Know

Common Questions

How much notice do you need?
At least 2 full days before your event. The order form won't let you pick a date sooner than that. For weddings and events over 50 guests, please give us as much notice as you can so we can plan properly.
Is there a minimum order?
Yes — 15 guests. We're a small home-style kitchen and we're not able to accommodate orders below that at this time, including à la carte trays and rice boxes. If you're planning something smaller, message us anyway and we'll let you know if that changes.
Do I pay when I submit the order?
No. The form sends us a request, not a payment. We'll contact you to confirm the date, the final quote and how you'd like to pay.
Are the prices on this site final?
They're an estimate. The totals shown don't include HST or delivery, and large or custom orders may be quoted differently. You'll always get the final number from us before anything is confirmed.
Where do you deliver?
We're based in Mississauga and serve the Greater Toronto Area. Delivery fees depend on distance — tell us your address and we'll include it in the quote.
Is the food halal?
Yes. All our meat is halal, and everything is cooked home-style in small batches.
Can you adjust spice levels or handle allergies?
Absolutely — just note it in the "Anything else?" box on the order form and we'll work around it.
